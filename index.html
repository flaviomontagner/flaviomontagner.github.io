Validation is where I learned to judge. AI is where I use it.

I work from research and journey mapping to testing and measurable outcomes. My day job is telling a plausible experience from one that works, with real people, before it ships.

Now I also build. With AI coding agents, Claude Code and Codex among them, and MCP tools, I turn requirements into working software in days, and I’m exploring how to fold these agents into the classic design process without handing them the judgement. I call the method DesAI Thinking.
